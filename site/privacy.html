Privacy

This covers the LCForge app, the LCForge Chrome extension and this website.

The short version

LCForge collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics and no server of its own. Your problems, your code and your history stay on your computer, and nothing about you is sent to us or to anyone else.

The app

LCForge runs on your computer and keeps everything it saves — the problems you import, your solutions, your run history and your ember — in one folder there. Help → About in the app shows where. It talks only to itself, on a port that accepts connections from your own machine and no other.

Two things can come from the internet, and only to you:

  • When a problem's statement includes a diagram, the diagram is loaded from LeetCode's own servers, the same as on LeetCode. Those requests carry no referrer.
  • If you run the bare .jar, the code editor (Monaco) is loaded from the cdnjs content network. The installers carry their own copy and skip this.

Your code is compiled and run on your own computer. It is never uploaded.

The Chrome extension

The extension runs only on LeetCode problem pages. When you press its ⚡ button, it reads the problem you are viewing — its statement, examples, code template and method signature — through the same request LeetCode's own page makes, and hands it to the LCForge app on your computer (an address that starts http://127.0.0.1). That is all it does.

  • It sends nothing anywhere but the LCForge app on your own machine.
  • It does not read your cookies, your account details, your submissions or your browsing history.
  • It stores nothing, and does nothing until you press the button.

This website

This site is static pages, hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers. To point the download buttons at the newest version, your browser asks GitHub's public API which release is the latest; GitHub's own privacy statement covers that request, and the page that served you, as it does any visit to a GitHub Pages site. If you pick a theme here, it is remembered in your own browser's storage and nowhere else.

Questions

LCForge is open source, so every claim on this page can be checked against its code. If something here is unclear, open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
